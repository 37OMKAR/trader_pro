**Fundamental Analysis: Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)**

**1. Valuation Assessment**
- **P/E (25.0x):** TCS trades at a premium to its 5-year historical average (~23x) but is broadly in line with large-cap IT peers. Given the strong earnings growth, the premium is justified.
- **P/B (3.8x):** Reflects high return on equity (ROE) and asset-light model. This is reasonable for a company with consistent capital efficiency.
- **Growth vs. Valuation:** With Net Profit growth at 18.2%, the PEG ratio is ~1.37 (25.0 / 18.2). This is slightly above the ideal 1.0, indicating the stock is fairly valued to marginally expensive, not a bargain.

**2. Balance Sheet Strength**
- **Debt/Equity (0.35):** Conservative leverage. This is primarily operational (lease liabilities) rather than financial debt, indicating low solvency risk.
- **ROE (19.4%) & ROCE (22.1%):** Excellent capital efficiency. ROCE comfortably exceeds the cost of capital, and ROE is among the best in the sector.
- **Cash Position:** While not provided, TCS’s low debt and high profitability typically translate to a strong net cash position, supporting buybacks and dividends.

**3. Fundamental Rating: Bullish**
- **Rationale:** The combination of double-digit revenue/profit growth, high-teens ROE, and low leverage makes TCS a high-quality compounder. The valuation is not cheap, but it is supported by earnings momentum and a fortress balance sheet. Risks include currency headwinds and global IT spending slowdowns, but the current fundamentals outweigh these concerns.
