**Fundamental Analysis: Tata Motors Ltd (TATAMOTORS)**  
**Price:** ₹980.14 | **Sector:** Automobile  

---

### 1. Valuation Assessment  
- **P/E (25.0x):** At a premium to the 5-year historical average (~15–18x) and the broader auto sector (~20x). This reflects strong earnings momentum but leaves limited margin of safety.  
- **P/B (3.8x):** Elevated versus historical ~2.5–3.0x, indicating the market is pricing in sustained high returns.  
- **Growth-adjusted view:** With net profit growth of 18.2%, the PEG ratio is ~1.37 (25.0/18.2), which is slightly above the fair-value threshold of 1.0.  
- **Conclusion:** Valuation is **rich** on absolute terms, but justified if JLR’s margin resilience and domestic EV ramp-up sustain double-digit earnings growth. Not a value buy; it is a growth-priced stock.

---

### 2. Balance Sheet Strength  
- **Debt/Equity (0.35):** Healthy deleveraging post the earlier high-debt phase. Net automotive debt is now manageable, with strong free cash flow generation from JLR.  
- **ROE (19.4%) & ROCE (22.1%):** Excellent capital efficiency, above most domestic peers (Maruti ~15% ROE, M&M ~16%). Indicates robust value creation.  
- **Liquidity & Coverage:** Interest coverage is comfortable (>5x), and the company has adequate liquidity for capex (EV, CV cycle).  
- **Conclusion:** Balance sheet is **strong**—low leverage, high returns, and improving credit profile. No near-term solvency risk.

---

### 3. Fundamental Rating: **Bullish**  
**Rationale:**  
- Strong earnings growth (18.2%) backed by JLR’s premium mix and domestic CV upcycle.  
- High ROE/ROCE with low leverage = quality compounding.  
- Key risks: Rich valuation (P/E 25x) and cyclicality in global auto demand. However, fundamentals outweigh valuation concerns for a medium-term horizon.  

**Actionable view:** Accumulate on dips below ₹920–950 (near 22x forward earnings). Avoid chasing at current levels. Monitor JLR order book and EV margin trajectory quarterly.
